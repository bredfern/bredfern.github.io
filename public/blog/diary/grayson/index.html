Grayson

1987-02-24

Grayson was an Asper, a person who is genetically engineered to use Psi powers. He was fleeing from Earth.

Yesterday I heard a noise. It was so loud it ruined my poise. I looked up from my studies and spied some pudgy buddies playing with their dirty toys.

"Get against the wall, Earth man,"

The Altrusian roughly pushed me up against the wall, although human the Altrusian

I never liked Altrusians. They looked like a cross between a blob of slime and a wart-hog and smelled like it too. They felt like the owned the galaxy.
