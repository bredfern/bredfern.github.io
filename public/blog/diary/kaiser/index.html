Kaiser

1990-09-25

Sherry and Geoff sat In the middle of The wide white room And slow swayed together To the sound of Stevie Nicks on some old Fleetwood Mac record From 1976,

They clapped when Stevie sang about the Witch, and Geoff stood up, said "Why is it all Kaiser Permanente, even Credit cards got magnetic strips on the backs?

Is it a conspiracy? Is the government Out to get me? I dunno."

and Sherry stood up said: "Wow."
