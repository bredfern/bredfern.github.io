Halloween

1986-10-28

Well, it has been a few days since I've written. I haven't done much, except get ready for Halloween. This year I was invited to a party at Mike Rupp's, but I declined, because I'm party sick. Also, in Theater Theater Class we are doing our plays in a real theater. One – a real theater. Unfortunately, I don't have that big of a part, because my character gets killed early on in the play.
