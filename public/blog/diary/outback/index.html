Outback

The next night the girl scouts and boy scouts were all making-out in a big shack. A beautiful blonde picked me up and said, "come on, lets go make-out with the rest."

But we were too late and the girl scout leader said that all of this would go on to not fucking go she made us stop, my we got home on a Monday. All of this took place in an Australian jungle.
