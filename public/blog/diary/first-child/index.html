First Child

1990-04-01

Imagine me, First child of my mother!

There would only be 3 laws. First, all fear would be abolished. Next all hatred would disappear, because no one would fear. Then finally all greed and ambition for power over one another would be done away with.

Greed's got nothing to do with money, It's all about power. Certain people, by nature or by upbringing, seem compelled to gain power over other people.

The world will never be a good, just place.
