1987-02-24

I haven't written for a long time, but I want to record the event that happened tonight.

It actually snowed! For the first time in 11 years, it snowed! We played in the snow and I hope it snows more so we don't have to go to school tomorrow.

Note: When writing the beginning of a story put the character in to a dangerous situation at first, but have him reflect on his past circumstances that led him to the present situation or at least or have another character or another narrator telling the story.

Instead of griping about the lyrics, just enjoy the music.
