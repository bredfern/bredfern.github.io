The Process

2026-08-21

Since the emergence of the figure of Richard Nixon we have also seen a concordant rise of chaos, poverty, misery and violence world wide. Such things as the "war on drugs" enabled the use of violence all around the world.

The poem Plutonian Ode by Allen Ginsberg captures the feel of the time which has continued to build and build to take us to the current state of emergency.

First penned unmindful by Doctor Seaborg with poisonous hand, named for Death's planet through the sea beyond Uranus whose chthonic ore fathers this magma-teared Lord of Hades,

Sire of avenging Furies, billionaire Hell- King worshipped once with black sheep throats cut, priests's face averted from underground mysteries in single temple at Eleusis,

Allen Ginsberg

This particular cthonic negative energy has had its play on the earth from roughly 1968 - 2028, with the energy of the serpent, destruction, toxicity, venom, fear, is replaced with the dove, kindness, compassion, teamwork and cooperation. Looking out for everyone, even the least.
