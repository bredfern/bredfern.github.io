Lean Meats

2025-01-20

A pipe that smokes itself Clocks so exhausted they droop Dying, time itself has expired

Her face removed for love But in horror there is Nothing but wires and Microchips in there

The alchemist cannot sleep Something is going wrong His pet ape has died
