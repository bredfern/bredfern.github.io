I got my first guitar made from carbon fiber and for metal these are really godhead, not just stability but a heavier tone than the heavist tone wood. Eyna guitars are basically making the world's best guitars from carbon fiber both electric and acoustic.

Carbon fiber guitars are so excellent.. With carbon fiber you can crank the distortion and fuzz all the way up and the pickups are naturally insulated by the carbon fiber and you get all the roar with none of the hiss. Then the tuning stability is crazy, we get some weather changes that make most guitar cry out in pain, but without any wood its crazy like a guitar to play on a space station.
